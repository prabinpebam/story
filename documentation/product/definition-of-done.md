## 1. The destination

### 1.1 The product we are finishing

**Story is the professional environment in which a team can design with Figma-class precision, produce and deliver with PowerPoint-class completeness, and maintain one governed narrative system across audiences, collaborators, devices, and outputs.**

The primary customer is a design-led team that repeatedly adapts consequential presentations: launches, customer narratives, executive reviews, consulting engagements, training, research, and conference work. The important advantage is not merely that drawing and presenting happen in one application. It is that reuse, legitimate variation, review, delivery, and exchange preserve one understandable authored source.

At completion, a designer, author, reviewer, and presenter can take realistic work from a blank or imported document to a reusable presentation system, create materially different audience editions, change shared sources, reconcile exceptions, collaborate, rehearse, record, deliver, and exchange the result without rebuilding content in another tool.

### 1.2 What world-class means here

World-class requires all of the following together:

| Dimension | What has to be true |
|---|---|
| Capability | Neither professional visual design nor professional presentation production has a missing essential workflow within the accepted scope. |
| Coherence | One understandable product, canonical document, mutation model, and fidelity contract connect all surfaces. |
| Craft | The interface, interactions, defaults, content, and outputs withstand expert review in ordinary and difficult states. |
| Trust | Accepted work survives, permissions mean what they say, private content stays private, and success indicators correspond to real outcomes. |
| Fluency | Experienced users transfer their knowledge; new users can complete useful work without task-specific coaching. |
| Differentiation | Governed multi-audience work is measurably better than the strongest relevant alternative workflow. |
| Inclusivity | Supported assistive technology, input, language, and device workflows are complete rather than afterthoughts. |
| Professional delivery | The application remains predictable in a room, offline, under load, during interruptions, and across actual output consumers. |
| Sustainability | The product can be deployed, supported, recovered, upgraded, and maintained without depending on its original implementer. |
| Evidence | Every claim is tied to a candidate, scope, environment, protocol, and independently inspectable result. |

These are conjunctive obligations. Excellent visuals cannot compensate for data loss. Broad features cannot compensate for inaccessible workflows. Passing tests cannot compensate for unusable interaction. Strong adoption cannot compensate for inaccurate fidelity claims.

### 1.3 The guarantee this document can and cannot provide

Completion provides a **versioned assurance that Story satisfies its complete accepted product contract and the comparative, craft, and sustained-use gates defined here**.

It cannot honestly guarantee that no undiscovered defect exists, that every person will prefer Story, that every competitor has been evaluated, or that Story will remain the best forever. No finite checklist can establish those claims.

The defensible leadership claim is narrower and stronger than marketing language:

> On the named candidate, supported environments, representative workloads, and dated comparison panel, Story met the full accepted product contract, met all noncompensable quality gates, and demonstrated the specified advantage for governed professional presentation work.

If that comparison has not been performed, the product may eventually be specification-conformant, but this document's world-class destination is **not achieved**.

`DOD-01` through `DOD-24` below identify acceptance bundles, not new atomic requirement namespaces. They provide stable planning references. Each bundle must map to exact existing `REQ-*`, `AC-*`, and applicability cells before it receives completion credit.

The additional claim gates in Sections 8, 9, and 14 are **proposed stricter acceptance obligations**, not already accepted requirements or current capabilities. Before this document can authorize a completion claim:

1. Product approves this document's outcome scope and named accountable people.
2. Each bundle and acceptance journey is mapped to exact owning requirements and cells.
3. New obligations are decomposed into atomic requirements by the existing owner; existing obligations retain their IDs.
4. The relevant owners adopt the added metrics, protocols, fixture obligations, and sign-off rules.
5. The accepted specification revision, profile/universe pair, and this document revision are bound together.
6. Quality confirms that no unmapped clause, unresolved conflict, blank threshold, or circular prerequisite remains.

This work is part of execution, not a reason to abandon the document. Until adoption is complete, its additions remain open work and cannot create passing evidence.

### 2.3 Finite scope without artificial incompleteness

The destination includes every active requirement in the accepted end-state specification, including the full design, presentation, Story-native, accessibility, trust, output, and operating-model obligations.

It does not require copying every feature, proprietary extension, UI label, or pixel of every competing application. It does not require a universal spreadsheet, video editor, website builder, or mobile precision-design suite. Optional AI, extensions, live data, and audience services cannot hold the core product hostage, but all applicable requirements apply whenever they are shipped or claimed.

The end-state support profile freezes browser/OS versions, hardware classes, device tiers, languages, inputs, assistive technologies, output consumers, limits, and enabled features. It cannot exclude a mandatory owner or required workflow to improve completion numbers.

An unknown external format construct can legitimately be preserved without editability when the normative fidelity contract allows it. That is different from downgrading a required supported workflow because implementation is missing. Native editable content and mandated interoperable feature families cannot be hidden behind a blanket raster fallback.

### 2.4 Change control

Freeze a baseline that can actually be finished. New unrelated ideas enter a reviewed successor specification; they do not silently expand today's workload. Discoveries necessary to satisfy an existing requirement remain defects or missing implementation, not optional future scope.

A material change versions the document and the affected normative owners, creates the required successor profile/universe pair, preserves prior evidence, and reopens affected cells. Neither deleting failed tasks nor refreshing a baseline onto a weaker implementation is acceptable.

## 3. Current-state assessment

### 3.1 Method and limits

This assessment is dated September 8, 2026 and bound to commit `42133ef3c0501374a4dba2ac28d0299929904910`. It uses production-source call-path review, representative test inspection, current generated registries, the existing specification, and the repository's existing specification/universe validation command.

This is not a complete execution of every workflow, a browser/physical-device/assistive-technology certification, a production reliability study, or an independent penetration test. Source review can establish a routed foundation or concrete integration gap; it cannot establish a release-wide pass. Historical test reports are not silently adopted as evidence for this candidate.

The [July 10 capability audit](capability-audit.md) is useful historical context, not the current source of truth. In particular, later shell/context and performance-gate work must not be described as still wholly absent.

### 3.2 Executive assessment

**Story has a substantial design-first slide-editor foundation, but is not yet a complete professional presentation product or a demonstrated governed narrative-systems product.**

The core problem is not a lack of ambition or specification breadth. It is the distance between substantial individual subsystems and one durable, coherent, accessible, measurable, end-to-end product.

The most consequential completion risks are shared-model fragmentation, incomplete production wiring, missing high-value authoring/output families, and a gap between verification infrastructure and release-admissible evidence. These need resolution before more surface area can reliably translate into user value.

## 4. The exact completion rule

### 4.1 Completion is a conjunction

```text
WorldClassDone(candidate, acceptedScope, evidenceDate) =
    AdoptionComplete
    AND FullAcceptedSpecificationClosed
    AND EveryRequiredApplicabilityCellPassing
    AND EveryProductBundleAndJourneyClosed
    AND QualityAndFidelityBudgetsPassing
    AND CraftAndHumanFluencyPassing
    AND ComparativeLeadershipPassing
    AND SustainedUseAndOperationalAcceptancePassing
    AND IndependentAssurancePassing
    AND NoUnresolvedConformanceOrCraftDefect
    AND NoHardZeroBreach
    AND NoActiveWaiverOrQuarantinedRequiredGate
    AND ExactReleaseDossierApproved
```

The implementation plan's eight ratios remain authoritative: specification, implementation, cell, protocol, evidence, artifact, quality, and decision closure. All must reach 100%; their minimum, not their mean, is the program score.

The added world-class gates are independently pass/fail and, once adopted, belong in the same traceability system. They cannot be averaged into a weighted score that conceals a failure.

### 4.2 Non-negotiable zeroes

Within every required conformance corpus, fault schedule, and claim-supporting study:

| Zero-tolerance result | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Silent authored-content loss, corrupt admission, or identity/order corruption | No feature or release can compensate. |
| Lost acknowledged operation or divergent accepted collaborative state | Network difficulty is not an excuse for false acknowledgment. |
| Presenter-private, other-edition, credential, or unauthorized content disclosure | Visibility in the correct view is not enough; inappropriate views must not receive the data. |
| Uninitialized/partial audience frame or reordered required build | Readiness and degradation must act before reveal. |
| False saved, synchronized, accessible, editable, compatible, or valid-output claim | A truthful blocked state is preferable to false success. |
| Inaccessible only path for a required workflow | A mouse workaround does not close an accessibility obligation. |
| Missing evidence disguised as pass, not applicable, or a successful retry | Completion accounting must fail closed. |
| Unresolved in-scope conformance or craft defect at final acceptance | Known defects cannot be relabeled enhancements to claim perfection. |

These are zero observed breaches under specified evidence, not a mathematical assertion about all possible future executions.

### 4.3 No convenient escape hatches

Missing, partial, unknown, not evaluated, blocked, failing, stale, expired, waived, quarantined, and preview are not done. A legitimate normative `not-applicable` requires review; it cannot mean "not implemented" or "we lack a test device."

A capability is not done because its model, menu, unit tests, screenshot, or README exists. A public feature flag is not an acceptance result. Required workflows must be reachable in the signed production configuration without developer tools, private storage flags, state injection, or undocumented setup.

## 9. Craft, usability, and comparative leadership

### 9.1 The comparison baseline must reflect the actual category

Official documentation consulted for this review shows that Figma Slides already connects slide and design modes, reusable library assets, collaboration, and presenter notes [EXT-01], and documents PPTX/PDF export with specific limitations [EXT-02]. Google documents accessible authoring/presentation paths and offline saving [EXT-05, EXT-06]. Canva documents presentation, collaboration, presenter, and output workflows [EXT-07].

Therefore, "a browser design tool with slides" is not sufficient differentiation. Story must be compared with realistic combined workflows, not an artificially weak slide editor or an obsolete version.

Before a summative comparison, assess Figma Design, Figma Slides, Microsoft 365 desktop PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Canva, and relevant contemporary challengers selected by the research owner. Record why each belongs or does not belong in a task-family comparison. Include the strongest relevant single-product or normal multi-tool workflow; do not force competitors to use duplicate decks when they provide a better native method.

The existing manual-duplicate benchmark remains useful for its constitutional efficiency measure. It is not sufficient by itself to establish leadership over current alternatives.

### 9.2 Existing product gates remain mandatory

Retain the complete 12 Figma-transfer, 14 PowerPoint-transfer, and 8 Story-native tasks, the defined fixture family, qualification rules, at least 12 non-employee participants per summative cohort, counterbalanced comparison, objective semantic validators, consent, failure retention, and all `SLO-01-001..010`.

| Provider expiry, permission denial, conflict, throttling, account switch | No overwrite of a newer/foreign file; local recoverability and honest cloud status. |
| Multi-tab concurrency, service-worker/cache version skew | No two uncoordinated authoritative writers or mixed incompatible document/runtime state. |
| Missing/corrupt/font/media/linked asset, CORS or codec denial | Correct warning/fallback/block before output or audience reveal; original content and references preserved. |
| Quota, disk-full, large archive, huge image, recursive graph, excessive queue | Bounded admission/backpressure and cleanup; no partial authoritative mutation or corrupt final artifact. |
| Source update during local/remote override editing | Correct scope, conflict/orphan classification, preserved intent, and invalidated readiness/approval. |
| Role downgrade/revoke while offline or during an output request | Privileged action denied at the actual authority; explicit treatment of recoverable local work and already distributed artifacts. |
| Unsupported/future package parts or format versions | Safe preservation or read-only/block outcome; no misleading editability or conversion guarantee. |
| Popup denial, display hotplug, wrong screen, closed audience window | Private controls never exposed; last valid content and exact session position recoverable. |
| Sleep, timer throttling, rapid navigation, seek/back during motion | Deterministic semantic command order and timeline state; no duplicated/omitted build. |
| Microphone/camera removal, denied consent, recording cancellation | Live tracks stop as required; capture state is truthful; recoverable segments are not falsely finalized. |
| RTL/IME/AT/zoom/forced-color/reduced-motion boundary | Complete equivalent task path and semantics; no inaccessible only route or corrupted text. |
| Hostile import/code/prompt/external relationship | No unauthorized execution, capability escalation, outbound content, cross-origin escape, or resource exhaustion beyond policy. |
| Failed/canceled batch export or publication | Per-artifact truthful result; no partial file labeled successful; no private edition included. |
| Telemetry/diagnostic failure or collection denial | Core local work and delivery continue; no implicit consent or raw content capture. |
| Upgrade/rollback/service outage/operator error | Tested compatible recovery path, retained evidence, bounded data-loss promise, and user-visible incident state. |

For each condition, test interruption immediately before and after every relevant acknowledgment/commit/finalization boundary. Include stale, duplicated, reordered, and delayed callbacks, not only cleanly rejected promises.

## 14. Operational and sustained-use acceptance

### 14.1 Existing reliability obligations

Volume 14 already defines rolling 30-day targets: >= 99.9% healthy authoring sessions, >= 99.95% healthy presentation sessions, >= 99.99% eligible local checkpoints, >= 99.9% eligible native saves, >= 99.5% supported output jobs, and >= 99.9% eligible collaboration acceptance. Their exact denominators, exclusions, error-budget policy, and hard-zero conditions apply.

Normal local recovery has the specified five-second loss bound under available storage; acknowledged shared operations have RPO 0. Do not conflate local preview, local durable recovery, provider save, and service acknowledgment. Regional collaboration recovery and all other RTOs retain their owning definitions.

Zero observed failures in a tiny pilot cannot substantiate a 99.95% reliability claim. Publish eligible event counts, failure classification, uncertainty, and study limits. External provider denial and user cancellation are separately recorded, not opportunistically used to shrink the denominator.

### 14.2 Proposed longitudinal world-class gate

After the product is safe and eligible for the applicable external tier, run a consented study with at least 10 independent target teams for at least 30 consecutive days. Each team completes at least three recurring presentation cycles and at least one real shared-source update across two materially different editions.

The proposed completion gate requires:

1. At least eight teams complete their assigned end-to-end work without a maintainer operating the product for them.
2. At least eight teams use the base/edition model again after the initial study task rather than abandoning it for unmanaged copies.
3. Every discovered in-scope conformance, trust, accessibility, and craft defect is terminally resolved with candidate-relevant evidence before final acceptance.
4. All exactness, privacy, durability, and safety hard-zero conditions hold; a breach triggers the owning incident/no-go process.
5. Support interactions, failures, workarounds, abandonments, manual repairs, and fallback to other tools are recorded, not removed from success rates.
6. Product/engineering review confirms that the actual customer work resembles the intended wedge rather than a curated demo.
7. The applicable rolling reliability objectives have valid denominators and evidence; insufficient event volume remains inconclusive and requires a longer observation period or a separately reviewed evidence plan, never a fabricated pass.

This study establishes repeated utility. It does not substitute for controlled comparative testing, real artifact/AT evidence, or the complete conformance corpus.

## 15. Final acceptance and continued assurance

### 15.1 Required release dossier

The dossier contains the exact signed candidate and reproducible build; accepted specification and DoD revisions; active immutable profile/universe pair; complete bundle/journey/requirement mappings; all eight closure views; current protocol/fixture/environment registries; raw and reviewed evidence; independent artifact/consumer reports; hardware/AT/fault results; quality distributions; human/comparative/craft/longitudinal reports; the append-only finding and decision registries; and deployment, monitoring, support, rollback, and recovery material.

Every required reviewer can locate a claimed result, inspect its source evidence, reproduce its derivation, and identify its limitations. No final dossier may mix unrelated candidates without a reviewed unchanged-semantics/evidence-adoption decision allowed by Volume 15.

### 15.2 Final decision procedure

1. Freeze one candidate, production configuration, accepted scope, and requested claim.
2. Reconcile every expected ID and close every required atomic/applicability/artifact/quality/decision obligation.
3. Close all 24 bundles, all required journeys, and the adopted additional claim gates through mapped evidence.
4. Independently rerun critical protocols, inspect actual output consumers, and witness physical/AT, Prolonged, recovery, and rollback evidence.
5. Confirm zero unresolved in-scope conformance/craft defects, active waivers, required quarantines, stale evidence, blank obligations, and unresolved or unreviewed decision-trigger states. A reviewed, evidenced `not-triggered` decision remains legitimate.
6. Confirm applicable R2 Supported and R3 Assured gates, including independent authority and exact evidence freshness.
7. Obtain explicit approval from every required role. One required no-go stops the decision; abstention is not approval.
8. Publish the exact support/fidelity/compatibility limitations and the bounded dated claim, never an unqualified "perfect."
9. Complete the owning post-release monitoring period and evaluate every hard-zero trigger immediately.
10. Record `achieved` only when the complete predicate in Section 4 holds; otherwise record the precise open obligations.

One person may perform multiple ordinary roles when the project is small, but the required independent assurance cannot be satisfied by the same implementer approving their own work. Lack of a reviewer, test environment, or event denominator is an open dependency.

### 15.3 When the world-class claim reopens

Reopen affected acceptance on a relevant product/schema/provider/browser/security change, a disproved invariant, a current customer failure, a material unsupported environment finding, stale evidence, invalidated comparative result, or failed rolling reliability budget.

Preserve historical results rather than editing them. Narrow or suspend current claims, protect existing documents, fix the problem, and collect fresh scoped evidence. A security, privacy, content-loss, audience, or artifact-integrity breach follows the immediate nonwaivable response.

The program is finished for an exact accepted baseline, not exempt from maintenance. The lasting standard is a product and engineering system capable of continuing to earn the claim.
